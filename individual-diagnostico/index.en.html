Before you begin

This isn't a diagnosis. No one is going to tell you who you are — this is only here to help you recognize, more clearly, patterns you may already sense but have never seen written down.

Personality isn't the same as rigidity. A trait isn't a life sentence — it's just the path that, through repetition, has become the easiest one to walk so far. Everything mapped out here can be refined, reworked, grown. None of it is fixed forever.

For this mirror to reflect well, answer honestly — not with the answer that sounds "best" or most flattering. There's no right answer here, only the one that's true for you, today.

There are about 50 questions, and it takes around 15-20 minutes. Nothing is saved on any server: everything happens right here, in your browser.
